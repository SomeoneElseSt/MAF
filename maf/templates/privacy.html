Privacy

Short version: MAF keeps your posts and replies, and almost nothing about you.

What we store

  • Posts, replies and edits. The words you write, with the thread they belong to and a random name per thread.
  • Private replies. Only the encrypted form. Your browser encrypts them before sending, so the server stores text it can't read.
  • A sign-in cookie. It holds your pass, signed. It's what keeps you signed in, and it's the only cookie MAF sets.
  • Counts. Views, flags and reactions, tied to your pass, not to anything else about you.

What we don't store

  • Your email. It's used once to send a code and then forgotten. Sign-in codes live in memory and expire.
  • IP addresses or request logs. Neither the app nor the web server in front of it keeps them.
  • Tracking or analytics. There are none. Your theme, drafts, pins and what you've seen in the Feed stay in your browser.

Why we keep it

To run the forum: showing threads, counting views and comments, hiding flagged posts, and keeping you signed in. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of you.

How long

Posts and replies stay until they're deleted (yours, or by the maintainer after a moderation request or flags). Deleted words are overwritten on disk, not kept. If you delete your account's content, nothing ties the rest back to you.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your own replies and edit your own posts at any time. For anything else (a post you want removed, or a question about what's stored), write to {{ org.contact_email }}.

Who runs it

MAF is run by students for {{ org.community }}. Questions go to {{ org.contact_email }}.

The code is public: github.com/SomeoneElseSt/MAF (opens in a new tab). You can check each claim above yourself in it.
