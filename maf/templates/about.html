About

An anonymous forum for Minerva University students.

Today the main channel is one Telegram group shared by every class. Each message notifies about a thousand people under your real name, so people post less and hold back unpopular opinions. MAF is a place to exchange ideas without that cost.

You verify once with your @uni.minerva.edu email. After that your device holds an anonymous pass. The email is only a gate: it's never stored, and it can't be linked to anything you post, including by whoever runs the server.

Read the User Guide for how anonymity works here.
