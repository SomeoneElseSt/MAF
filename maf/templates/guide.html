User Guide

Anonymity

You get a random name in each thread and a different one in the next, and your email is never stored. The university can still see a code email reached you, and timing can give clues.

Rules

Don't name students or staff. Five flags hide a post, and anyone can then ask for it back or for it to be removed.

Making a post

Click Submit and add a title plus a link or some text; hashtags are optional. Posts can't be edited afterwards.

Private replies

Tick "Make only visible to …" under a comment box to reply to just that person. Threads of private replies only appear in the device you were signed on when you made them.

Hashtags

#help, #wondering, #confession and #hottakes fill in a template for your post and its replies; on #hottakes, argue with the take, not the person.
