User Guide

Anonymity

You get a random name in each thread to avoid traceability across posts. Your email is never stored and only used for verification using blind signatures.

Rules

{% if org.naming_rule %}Don't name students or staff. {% endif %}Be respectful. Five flags on a post hide it. A hidden post can be restored or deleted permanently on a case-by-case basis.

Making a post

Click Submit and add a title plus a link or some text; hashtags are optional. You can edit a post's text afterwards (its title, link and hashtags stay as they were), and the same goes for your replies. Anything you're halfway through writing is kept in your browser (never sent anywhere) until it posts, so a reload or an outage doesn't lose it.

Private replies

Tick "Make only visible to …" under a comment box to reply to just that person. Your browser encrypts the reply before sending it, so {{ org.site_name }}'s server only stores scrambled text it can't read. Private replies only appear on the device you were signed in on when you made them, and they need JavaScript.

One limit: {{ org.site_name }}'s server also sends the page's code, so someone in control of the server could change that code to copy replies before they're encrypted. Encryption protects against anyone reading the stored database or its backups.

Dates

Dates is a page of its own, kept apart from the rest of {{ org.site_name }}: its posts never show on the front page, in hashtags or in search. Your profile is a few broad answers (where you're from, gender, a height range, your rotation city and what you're looking for) plus an optional comment. You can have one profile, and you can edit or delete it at any time.

To show interest in someone, reply to their profile with yours. Only they see it. If they match with you, private messages open between you two in their thread. They're encrypted like private replies, so they show only on the device you used. Either of you can unmatch, which erases the messages for both of you and can't be undone. The owner of a profile can also hide a profile someone sent them.

The answers are broad on purpose, but in a small community people may still guess who you are. Once you match, share your name or contact if you want to.

Hashtags

#help, #wondering, #confession and #hottakes suggest a template for your post and its replies. #hottakes posts are highlighted in orange on the main page.

Developers may choose to read {{ org.site_name }} programmatically.
