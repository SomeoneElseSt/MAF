User Guide

Anonymity

You get a random name in each thread and a different one in the next, so nobody can link your posts. Your email is never stored.

Making a new post

Click Submit, add a title and a link or some text. Posts can't be edited afterwards.

What is required and not

A title, plus a link or text. Hashtags are optional.

Commenting under a post

Write under the post, or click Reply under a comment. Tick "Make only visible to …" to reply privately to just that person.

Reacting to a post or comment

Click the smiley to add an emoji, or click an existing one to add or remove yours.

Hashtags

Add up to 3. #help, #wondering, #confession and #hottakes fill in a template to guide your post and replies.

The rule

Don't name students or staff. Public figures are fine.

Flags

Five flags hide a post from the front page. Anyone can then ask for it back or for it to be removed.

What can be seen

The university can see a code email reached you. Timing and shared networks can still give clues.

New device

Sign in again with your email. There's no password.

Using the data

You can read MAF from your own tools. Use this data explains how.
