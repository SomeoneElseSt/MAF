How it works

A place to say what you think without a thousand notifications and your name attached.

Your email is a gate, not an identity

You sign in with your university email so only students get in. The site uses it once to send a code, then forgets it. It's never saved, and nothing you post can be traced back to it, not even by the people who run MAF.

IDs change in every thread

Inside a thread you get a random ID like quiet-otter, so people can follow the conversation. In the next thread you get a different one. There are no profiles and no post history, so nobody can piece together who you are.

One rule

Don't name students or staff. If a post looks like it names someone, you'll be asked to check before it goes up. Public figures are fine.

Flags

If 5 people flag a post, it's hidden from the main page and search. Anyone with the link can still see it and ask for it to be brought back or removed for good.

What we can and can't see

  • The university can see that a code email was sent to you, because it runs your inbox. That's all.
  • MAF sees posts and anonymous IDs, never emails or IP addresses.
  • Known limits: the timing of posts and shared networks can give clues, and you're trusting that the site runs its published code. The source is public so anyone can check.

Switching devices

Just sign in again with your email. There are no passwords to remember.
