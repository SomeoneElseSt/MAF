About

What?

An anonymous forum for Minerva students.

Why?

Most of our conversation happens in one big group chat, where every message notifies about a thousand people and carries your name. That makes it easy to stay quiet, especially about anything unpopular. MAF is a place to post the thought anyway.

How?

You sign in once with your university email, which is never stored. Posts carry a random ID that changes in every thread. A student runs it, not the university, and the code is open source so anyone can check how anonymity works.
