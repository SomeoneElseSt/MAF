Link preview card: five versions

When a reply links to a MAF thread, the reply shows a card for it: the title, the first two lines of the post, and its details. Each version below sits in a real reply so you can judge it in place. Tell me a number and I'll build it.

Version 1 · The plain bordered card

A quiet box with the thread's own look inside: serif title and text, sans details. Closest to "the thread, small".
